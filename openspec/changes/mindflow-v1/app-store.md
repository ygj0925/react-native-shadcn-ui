## Full Description

### Chinese

MindFlow 是你的 AI 个人效率助手，将任务、笔记、日程和习惯追踪整合在一个优雅的本地优先应用中。

主要功能：
- 任务管理：创建、追踪和完成每日任务
- 富文本笔记：记录想法，AI 自动摘要与分类
- 日程规划：月视图日历，关联任务与事件
- 习惯追踪：连续打卡、统计图表、目标达成
- AI 助手：自然语言创建任务/事件，智能洞察周报
- 订阅升级：Pro 计划解锁无限 AI、笔记和习惯

数据默认存储在本地，登录后可同步到云端，支持离线使用和跨设备访问。

### English

MindFlow is your AI-powered personal productivity assistant, bringing tasks, notes, calendar and habit tracking into one elegant, local-first app.

Key features:
- Task management: create, track and complete daily tasks
- Rich notes: capture ideas with AI summary and auto-categorization
- Calendar planning: monthly view with linked tasks and events
- Habit tracking: streaks, charts and goal achievement
- AI assistant: create tasks/events in natural language, weekly insights
- Subscription upgrade: Pro plan unlocks unlimited AI, notes and habits

Data is stored locally by default and can be synced to the cloud after sign-in. Works offline and across devices.
